About

PetriRL Studio is part of the PetriRL framework, which combines colored timed Petri nets with reinforcement learning to schedule discrete event systems, from the job shop to flexible manufacturing.

A demonstrator

PetriRL Studio is the pilot of a PhD project, not a product, and comes without warranty. One job computes at a time; uploads and results are deleted within 24 hours (Privacy).

How it works

The Petri net is a light digital twin of the shop. Its token flow makes every decision traceable, its guard functions mask the actions that are not feasible, and the agent is only called when a decision is possible (event-based reinforcement learning). Dispatching rules, metaheuristics and learned agents all act on the same net, so their results compare directly and every plan can be replayed step by step.

The framework also covers intralogistics with AGVs and tool sharing, hyper-heuristics, dynamic job shops with machine breakdowns, and action masking.
